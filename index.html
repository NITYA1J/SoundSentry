Why nobody believed you

The inspector measured the tip

A city sound meter uses A-weighting, a filter that removes most low-frequency sound. The reading comes out small and passes. FenceLine also records the C-weighted level, which keeps the low end.

Drag the slider. When the C-weighted level runs more than 20 dB above the A-weighted level, low-frequency sound is dominating what you live with.

What it is

Your hum has a name

A number like 117 Hz means little to most people. A note is something you can hum back, describe to a neighbor, and bring to a council meeting.

Press the button to hear the measured tone. Tap other keys to compare it with nearby notes.

Is the data solid?

Before customer use

  • Verify thresholds against sources: the WHO night guideline, the 20 dB LCeq minus LAeq rule of thumb, and vibration perception levels.
  • Ripples need one node deliberately near the data center and one far or reference node.
  • The B♭ tone is synthesized in the browser. It is not a recording.
  • All figures on this page are sample data and should be replaced with packet metrics.
